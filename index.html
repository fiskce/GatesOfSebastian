How to play

Pay anywhere. No paylines: land 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 6×5 grid to win.

Tumble. Winning symbols shatter, the rest fall and new ones drop in. Tumbling repeats while new wins form, so one spin can chain many wins.

Multiplier orbs. On any winning tumble, Sebastian can fire an orb worth one of these:

Orbs fly into the multiplier collector. When the tumbling stops, its total multiplies the spin's win.

Free spins. 4+ scatters award 15 free spins. In the feature, every orb is saved into a total multiplier that applies to every winning spin until the feature ends. 3+ scatters in the feature add +5 spins.

Double chance (ante). +25% stake for about 1.7× the chance to trigger free spins naturally. Buy free spins for 100× the bet (ante must be off).

Tips. Tap the board or press Space during a spin to hurry it. Space spins, ↑/↓ change the bet, T toggles turbo, M mutes.
